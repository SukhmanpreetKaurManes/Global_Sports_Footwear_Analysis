# Global-Sports-Footwear-Analysis
# 👟 Global Sports Footwear Analysis

> **An end-to-end Business Intelligence project using Python and Power BI to analyze global sports footwear sales, customer behavior, pricing strategies, market opportunities, and product performance.**

---

## 📖 Project Overview

Businesses generate vast amounts of transactional sales data every day, but extracting meaningful insights from this data remains a challenge. This project demonstrates how data analytics can transform raw sales records into actionable business intelligence through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), interactive dashboards, and strategic recommendations.

Using Python for data analysis and Power BI for dashboard development, this project evaluates brand performance, customer behavior, pricing efficiency, category trends, sales channels, and geographic performance to support data-driven business decisions.

---

# 📌 Business Problem

The global sports footwear industry is highly competitive, with multiple brands competing across different countries, product categories, customer segments, and sales channels. While organizations collect large volumes of sales data, decision-makers often struggle to identify:

- Which brands generate sustainable revenue while maintaining pricing efficiency?
- How discounts influence customer purchasing behavior and profitability.
- Which product categories drive business growth versus profit margins.
- Which countries represent the strongest opportunities for expansion.
- Whether customer demographics significantly affect purchasing behavior.
- How effectively Online and Retail channels contribute to business performance.
- Which strategic initiatives should be prioritized to maximize long-term growth.

Without a data-driven approach, pricing decisions, promotional campaigns, inventory planning, and market expansion strategies may rely on assumptions rather than business evidence.

This project addresses these challenges by combining Python-based exploratory data analysis with an interactive Power BI dashboard that converts sales data into meaningful business insights.

# 📈 Key Business Insights

## 🏆 Brand Performance

- ASICS emerged as the highest revenue-generating brand while maintaining premium pricing with moderate discounting.
- Nike demonstrated the strongest pricing power by achieving the highest selling price with the lowest promotional dependency.
- Revenue distribution across brands remains highly competitive, indicating a balanced market.

---

## 💰 Pricing & Promotions

- Higher discount levels consistently reduced realized revenue per order.
- Customer satisfaction showed a moderate negative relationship with discount intensity.
- Brand value and product quality appear to influence customer satisfaction more than promotional pricing.

---

## 🌍 Market Performance

- UAE emerged as the strongest overall market with the highest revenue and customer satisfaction.
- Pakistan presents strong long-term growth potential due to high customer satisfaction despite comparatively lower revenue.
- USA requires strategic attention because of lower customer satisfaction and lower average order value.

---

## 🛒 Sales Channels

- Online and Retail channels contribute almost equally to overall revenue.
- The company demonstrates a balanced omnichannel strategy with no dependence on a single sales channel.

---

## 👟 Product Portfolio

- Lifestyle and Training categories deliver the strongest overall business performance.
- Basketball acts as a volume-driven growth category.
- Running and Gym present opportunities for portfolio optimization.

---

# 💡 Strategic Recommendations

- Increase investment in Training and Lifestyle categories.
- Reduce excessive discount dependency to improve profitability.
- Expand market penetration in Pakistan.
- Improve customer experience initiatives within the USA.
- Continue strengthening the balanced omnichannel strategy.
